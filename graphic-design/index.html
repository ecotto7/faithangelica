Graphic Design

Hey there, art aficionados! I'm Faith, an artist with a knack for adding a splash of uniqueness to my creations. I've been immersed in art classes since I was a kid, exploring various mediums like pencil portraits, clay, mosaics, and even finding solace in painting rocks (yes, I rock at it!). But the real fun started when I discovered the digital canvas of my iPad - it's been a total game-changer for my artistic adventures!

As a web designer, my artistic sensibilities come into play not only in creating visually stunning websites, but also in crafting graphics that pop and t-shirt designs that make a statement. Think of me as the Bob Ross of web design, channeling my inner artistic muse to create websites that are as creatively delightful as my many doodle-filled notebooks. Happy little websites, anyone?

But that's not all! My fiancé has an Etsy shop where you can check out some of my designs and see how my artistic flair extends beyond the digital realm. From quirky illustrations to bold graphics, my artistic touch is evident in every piece.

So, if you're looking to add a touch of artistic magic to your online presence or rock some one-of-a-kind t-shirt designs, I'm here to make it happen. Let's get artsy together and create something truly original!
