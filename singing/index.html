Singing

I've always had a special place in my heart for singing. It all started when I was a kid and joined the choir, and I kept singing throughout high school.

But then life took me on a different path, and I ended up living in Hawaii for a few years.

During my time in Hawaii, I took a break from singing, but I couldn't stay away from music for long. When I moved back to Tennessee, I found myself falling in love with music and singing all over again.

It was like rekindling a flame that had been burning inside me all along.

Since then, music has become a constant process in my life. It's a choice I make every day to pursue my passion for singing and immerse myself in the creative process. Whether it's writing my own songs, collaborating with other musicians, or working on my upcoming album,

it's a journey of self-discovery and expression.

I've learned that music is more than just a hobby or a pastime for me. It's a part of who I am, and it's something that brings me pure joy and fulfillment.

It's a constant reminder to follow my heart and pursue what truly resonates with my soul.
