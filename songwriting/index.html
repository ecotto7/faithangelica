Songwriting

I've been honing my craft since elementary school, when I first started writing poems and melodies. By high school, my passion for songwriting had fully blossomed, and I penned my first album in 2013. Another project followed in 2014, released a year later. My education in songwriting continued at MTSU, where I studied commercial songwriting and gained invaluable experience through various recording projects.

I'm proud to have earned my degree in commercial songwriting from there.

As I reflect on the past decade, I'm filled with excitement, knowing that it's been nearly ten years since my debut album.

Currently, I'm pouring my heart into my latest project, drawing inspiration from my experiences over the years.

In my pursuit of growth and learning, I've also been taking a class with fellow songwriters from Nashville and across the country, immersing myself in the intricacies of the music business, learning from successful industry insiders.

I'm always open to collaborations and co-writing opportunities, so feel free to hit me up. I would be thrilled to work with you and create something special together!
